**The case for retiring (one option, not a conclusion).**
- The two sets fail the product's own rule of one coherent face.
- It is the single strongest source of the "AI-made" impression (D-02).
- A consistent character needs either an illustrator (cost, approvals) or a tightly controlled generation process you approve; both are real work.
- One cheaper way to carry imagination: **objects from her real photographs** (duck, balloon, butterfly, marigold, flower crown, cake candle, kasavu border), drawn as simple flat shapes. They are clearly not documentary, and they are cheap to make and keep consistent (hand-authored SVG, a few lines each).

**If you want to keep a character:** keep **one** set only, redrawn so she is the same child in one style. Her age is your choice: her real age, or a clearly imaginary older Varada. Where she appears, including beside photographs, is a design decision to test in the prototype, not a rule.

## 4. The visual-proof pack (`docs/visual-proof/`)
All 11 files are concept boards in one generated style: cream, Fraunces, the painted baby, and board headlines such as "Warm foundation. Vivid chapters. Varada always remains the centre." (Observed.) They are the source of today's look, The temporary Home crops of July were cut from `typography-six-photo-stress-test.png` (PROJECT_STATUS §3.14). The full-size "enhanced" `home-1` and `home-5` that replaced them (§3.15) still carry that board's rounded frame (Observed), which indicates they were produced from those crops rather than from camera originals. That is an inference, and the owner can confirm it. **Disposition:** move them to `docs/visual-proof/archive-2026-07/` with a README line saying *superseded by owner instruction, 24 Sep 2026, kept for history*. Nothing should treat them as binding any longer ([A8](A8-plan-acceptance.md), document changes).
